Lennart Röstel

PhD candidate, TU Munich (AIDX Lab) · previously NVIDIA

I work on reinforcement learning, state estimation and sim-to-real transfer for robot hands. Most of my PhD has been about in-hand manipulation from touch alone. Our system was the first to reorient objects to a given goal orientation using only tactile sensing, with policies trained in simulation and run zero-shot on the real hand.

Last year I interned at NVIDIA, where I implemented SDF-hydroelastic contacts for the Newton simulator. Before the PhD I did a B.Sc. in Physics at CAU Kiel and an M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at TUM.

Selected work

Purely tactile in-hand manipulation

An RL policy reorients objects to a goal orientation without requiring vision, while a learned estimator tracks the object pose from tactile signals. Training estimator and policy together in realistic simulations makes the policy robust to partial observability, contributing to reliable zero-shot transfer to the real hand. A single policy generalizes this to many objects, including shapes not seen in training.
